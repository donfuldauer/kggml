# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Kggml project a harassment-free, respectful, and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and technical quality of the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via repository contact channels. All reports will be reviewed and addressed promptly and fairly.
